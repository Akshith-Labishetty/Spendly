# Future Ideas

Features and improvements to consider for future versions of Spendly.

## 1. Budget Limits
Set monthly budgets per category (e.g., Food: Rs.5000, Transport: Rs.2000). Show progress bars on the dashboard and warn when approaching or exceeding limits.

## 2. Recurring Transactions
Detect and auto-add recurring expenses like rent, subscriptions, EMIs. Let users mark a transaction as recurring with a frequency (weekly, monthly, yearly).

## 3. Multi-Currency Support with Live Rates
Fetch real-time exchange rates so users can log expenses in different currencies while viewing totals in their preferred currency.

## 4. Export to PDF/Excel
Generate downloadable monthly expense reports as PDF or Excel files with charts, category breakdowns, and transaction lists.

## 5. Shared Expenses / Split Tracking
Track expenses split between friends or roommates. Mark who paid, who owes, and settle balances.

## 6. Photo Receipt Scanner
Upload a photo of a receipt and use OCR (or manual entry assisted by image) to extract amount, date, and merchant name.

## 7. Savings Goals
Set financial goals (e.g., "Emergency Fund: Rs.1,00,000") and track progress with a visual progress bar showing how close you are.

## 8. Email/WhatsApp Import
Forward bank SMS or email statements to a specific address and auto-import transactions from them.

## 9. Dark/Light Theme Auto-Switch
Sync theme with the device's system preference and allow scheduling (e.g., dark mode after 8 PM).

## 10. PWA Support
Make Spendly installable as a Progressive Web App with offline access to cached transaction data and a native app-like experience on mobile.
